## 6. Key Info

- **IDX trading hours:** 09:00-15:00 WIB (UTC+7), Mon-Fri
- **IDX settlement:** T+2
- **Stock taxes:** No capital gains tax for Indonesian retail. Dividend tax (withholding): ~10-20%
- **US stock taxes (for Indonesian residents):** No capital gains tax. Dividend withholding: 15-30% (Indonesia-US tax treaty rate varies)
- **IDX brokers:** All BEI members trade the same ~900 stocks. Choose by fee structure, margin availability, and app quality
- **IBKR funding:** Wire USD from Indonesian bank (allow 3-5 business days, ~$10-30 fee per wire)
