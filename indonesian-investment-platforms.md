# Indonesian Investment Platforms (Simplified)

## 1. Forex Platforms

Most forex trading happens via **MetaTrader 4/5** brokers. These are the major ones accepting Indonesians:

### BAPPEBTI-Regulated (Local Legal)

| Platform | Regulation | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| Central Capital Futures | BAPPEBTI | Locals: Askap, Gatra, Monex, Soegee also available |
| Askap Futures | BAPPEBTI | Verify license status on bappebti.go.id |
| Monex Investindo Futures | BAPPEBTI | Licensed for forex, gold, indices |

Leverage typically capped at 1:100 for BAPPEBTI brokers. Pairs include majors + IDR crosses.

### Offshore (MT4/MT5, Accessible from Indonesia)

| Platform | Regulator | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| XM | CySEC, ASIC, IFSC | IDR account, tight spreads, leverage up to 1:888 |
| Exness | FSA Seychelles, CySEC, FCA | High leverage, IDR support |
| FBS | CySEC, IFSC | Active Indonesia IB network |
| IC Markets | ASIC, CySEC, FSA Seychelles | ECN pricing, tight spreads |
| OctaFX | FSA SVG | Was popular in Indonesia |
| HotForex (HFM) | FSCA, CySEC, FCA | Multi-regulation |

All offer EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AUD/USD, USD/IDR, etc. via MT4/MT5.

### Tradable Assets on IDX
- **Indonesian stocks** (~900 companies listed on BEI)
- **ETFs** (LQ45, IDX30, sector ETFs, feeder funds to S&P 500)
- **REITs** (several listed property trusts)
- **Warrants & Rights**
- **Index Futures** (LQ45 futures via derivatives exchange)

### Notes
- IDX opens 09:00-15:00 WIB (UTC+7)
- Settlement T+2
- No capital gains tax on stocks for Indonesian retail (tax only on dividends)
- Most brokers now offer online account opening via KTP + selfie

## 4. Foreign Stock Brokers

These allow Indonesian residents to trade US and global equities.

| Broker | Tradable Markets | Minimum Deposit | Key Fees | Notes |
|--------|----------------|----------------|---------|-------|
| **Interactive Brokers (IBKR)** | US, HK, EU, SG, UK, JP, AU, CA | ~$0 (no min for IBKR Lite) | Low; ~$0.35/order | Best value for global access. Supports USD wire from Indonesia |
| **Saxo Markets** | US, EU, HK, SG | ~$500 equiv | Higher than IBKR | Premium platform; advanced tools |
| **TD Ameritrade (via Schwab)** | US | $0 | $0 commission | Verify Indonesian residency acceptance |
| **Webull** | US | $0 | $0 commission | Verify KYC acceptance for Indonesia |
| **eToro** | US, EU (CFD structure) | $10-50 | Spread-based | Social trading; note CFD vs real share distinction |
| **Gotrade** | US (Fractional shares) | Low minimum | Small fx fee | Popular for fractional investing from SEA; waitlist model |
| **Pluang** | US (S&P 500 feeder fund) | IDR 10k | Management fee | Not direct stocks; mutual fund route to S&P 500 |
| **Bibit** | US (S&P 500 feeder fund) | IDR 10k | Management fee | Mutual fund route to S&P 500 |

### Key Info
- **IBKR** is the standard choice for serious investors. Direct market access, lowest fees, robust platform. Fund by wire transfer from Indonesian bank (may need to use intermediary/branch instructions).
- **Gotrade** and **Pluang/Bibit** suit small amounts but don't give direct stock ownership (feeder funds or fractional via custodian).
- No Indonesian broker offers direct US stock trading yet (some used to via partnership with US brokers but most stopped).
- US stocks: 15-30% withholding tax on dividends (treaty rate for Indonesia). No capital gains tax for non-US residents.

## Quick Summary: What Can You Trade from Indonesia?

| Asset | Best Entry Point | Difficulty |
|-------|-----------------|------------|
| **Forex** | MetaTrader (Exness, XM, IC Markets) | Easy |

| **US Stocks** | Interactive Brokers | Moderate (wiring funds) |
| **Global Stocks** | Interactive Brokers | Moderate |
| **Index ETFs** | IDX via Indonesian broker; US ETFs via IBKR | Easy |
| **Options/Futures** | Interactive Brokers (options), local for LQ45 futures | Harder |
