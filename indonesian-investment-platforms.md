# Indonesian Investment & Trading Platforms Research

**Last Updated:** June 2026  
**Scope:** Platforms accessible to Indonesian residents, organized by tradable asset class.  
**Note:** Fee structures, minimum deposits, and regulatory rosters change frequently. Verify current details on the official regulator websites (OJK, BAPPEBTI, BEI) and directly with each platform before opening an account.

## 2. Forex & CFDs

Forex trading in Indonesia is regulated by BAPPEBTI (commodity futures regulator). Be aware that many offshore brokers market to Indonesians but are not locally regulated.

### BAPPEBTI-Regulated Forex Brokers
These brokers hold a Pialang Berjangka (Futures Broker) license from BAPPEBTI.

| Platform | Regulation | Tradable Assets | Notes |
|----------|-----------|----------------|-------|
| **PT Central Capital Futures** | BAPPEBTI | Forex pairs, gold, silver, oil, indices | One of the older regulated names |
| **PT Askap Futures** | BAPPEBTI | Forex, gold, indices | Long-established local broker |
| **PT Monex Investindo Futures** | BAPPEBTI | Forex, gold, silver, oil, indices | Well-known in local retail market |
| **PT Gatra Berjangka / GIC Trade** | BAPPEBTI | Forex, gold, indices | Active marketing to retail segment |
| **PT International Mitra Futures (IMF)** | BAPPEBTI | Forex, commodities | Verify current license status on BAPPEBTI website |
| **PT Soegee Futures** | BAPPEBTI | Forex, gold, indices | Local presence |

**Typical BAPPEBTI Broker Terms (verify current rules):**
- Leverage: Historically capped at 1:100 for IDR accounts (verify current BAPPEBTI regulation)
- Spreads: Variable; local brokers may have wider spreads than offshore
- IDR pairs: USD/IDR, EUR/IDR, JPY/IDR, GBP/IDR often available
- Deposit: IDR bank transfer, often IDR 1-10 million minimum

### Offshore Brokers Accessible from Indonesia
Many Indonesians use offshore brokers for tighter spreads and higher leverage. These are NOT regulated by BAPPEBTI/OJK.

| Platform | Regulation | Notes |

**Risk Warning:** Offshore brokers operate outside Indonesian regulatory protection. If the broker fails, Indonesian regulators cannot assist in fund recovery. Use only well-capitalized, long-established offshore brands.

## 3. Cryptocurrency

Cryptocurrency trading in Indonesia is regulated by BAPPEBTI. Only BAPPEBTI-registered exchanges can legally facilitate crypto asset trading with IDR. International exchanges may be accessible but operate in a gray area for IDR fiat on/off ramps.

### BAPPEBTI-Registered Crypto Exchanges (Legal IDR Trading)
These are licensed as Pedagang Fisik Aset Kripto (Physical Crypto Asset Traders).

| Platform | Registered | Tradable Assets | Notes |
|----------|-----------|----------------|-------|
| **Indodax** | BAPPEBTI | BTC, ETH, USDT, XRP, ADA, DOGE, SOL, and 80+ others | Largest and oldest Indonesian crypto exchange; IDR fiat on/off ramp |
| **Tokocrypto (TokoCrypto)** | BAPPEBTI | BTC, ETH, BNB, USDT, and 100+ altcoins | Binance-backed; strong liquidity; IDR support |
| **Rekeningku** | BAPPEBTI | BTC, ETH, USDT, and various altcoins | Local player; IDR pairs |

| **Pluang Crypto** | BAPPEBTI | BTC, ETH, and curated assets | Part of Pluang multi-asset fintech; mobile app |
| **Triv** | BAPPEBTI | BTC, ETH, USDT, and others | Also supports bill payments with crypto |
| **Luno** | BAPPEBTI | BTC, ETH, XRP, LTC, BCH, USDC, USDT | Global brand with Indonesian entity |
| **Upbit Indonesia** | BAPPEBTI | BTC, ETH, and selected altcoins | Korean Upbit affiliate; verify current operations |

**Typical Fees:**
- Trading fee: ~0.1% - 0.3% per trade (maker/taker model common)

- Withdrawal: IDR bank transfer fees vary; crypto withdrawal fees vary by blockchain
- Minimum trade: Often as low as IDR 10,000 - 50,000

| **Bareksa** | Fund Marketplace + Bond Marketplace | Mutual funds, ORI, SBR, sukuk | OJK-recognized; also sells government bonds |

| **Direct from Asset Managers** | Official Channels | Manulife, Schroders, Eastspring, BNI-AM, Mandiri Investasi, Danamas, etc. | Often higher minimums (IDR 100k - 1M) but lower ongoing fees |

**Typical Fund Types Available:**
- **RDPU (Money Market):** Lowest risk, lowest return, high liquidity
- **RDPT (Fixed Income):** Government/corporate bond funds
- **RDS (Equity/Stock):** Invest in BEI-listed stocks; higher volatility
- **RDMC (Mixed/Balanced):** Blend of equity and fixed income
- **RDPU Syariah / RDS Syariah:** Sharia-compliant versions
- **Index ETFs:** Tracks IDX30, LQ45, or S&P 500 (via feeder funds)

**Typical Fees:**
- Subscription fee: 0% - 2% (many platforms now offer 0%)
- Redemption fee: 0% - 1% (often free after holding period)
- Management fee: 0.5% - 2% p.a. (embedded in NAV)
- Minimum investment: IDR 10,000 - IDR 100,000 on fintech platforms

## 5. Fixed Income & Bonds

### Government Bonds (SBN - Surat Berharga Negara)
| Bond Type | Issuer | Term | Interest | Accessible Via |
|-----------|--------|------|----------|---------------|
| **ORI (Obligasi Ritel Indonesia)** | Government | 3-10 years | Fixed coupon | Bareksa, e-SBN (Ministry of Finance), banks, some brokers |
| **SBR (Savings Bond Ritel)** | Government | 2 years | Floating coupon (benchmark + spread) | Bareksa, e-SBN, banks |
| **Sukuk Ritel (ST / SBSN)** | Government | Sharia-compliant | Profit-sharing rate | Same channels as ORI |
| **Surat Perbendaharaan Negara (SPN)** | Government | Short-term | Discount or coupon | Primary dealers, wholesale |
| **Obligasi Negara (ON)** | Government | Medium/long | Fixed/floating | Secondary market via brokers |

**Retail Platforms for SBN:**
- **e-SBN (Ministry of Finance):** Official government portal for ORI, SBR, Sukuk
- **Bareksa:** Popular fintech gateway for SBN; allows small lot purchases
- **Major Banks (BNI, Mandiri, BCA, BRI):** Treasury desks and online banking
- **Brokerages:** Some securities firms trade SBN in the secondary market

### Corporate Bonds
| Access Method | Notes |
|---------------|-------|
| **Direct from Issuer (Primary)** | Require larger minimums (IDR 100M-1B typically) |
| **Bareksa Secondary Market** | Some corporate bonds available in smaller lots |
| **Brokerages (Secondary OTC)** | OTC market via banks/brokers; wholesale focus |
| **Mutual Fund Route (RDPT)** | Easiest retail access via fixed-income mutual funds |

## 6. Commodities & Derivatives

### Commodity Futures Exchanges
| Exchange | Products | Regulator | Notes |
|----------|----------|-----------|-------|
| **Bursa Berjangka Jakarta (BBJ / JFX)** | Gold, forex, crude palm oil (CPO), olein, coffee, cocoa | BAPPEBTI | Indonesia's main futures exchange |
| **Indonesia Commodity & Derivatives Exchange (ICDX)** | Gold, silver, forex, CPO, tin, rubber | BAPPEBTI | Competing futures exchange |

**Commodity Products Available:**
- **Gold (Loco London / Rolling Spot):** Available via many BAPPEBTI brokers; most popular retail commodity
- **Crude Palm Oil (CPO):** Indonesia's flagship export commodity; futures contracts on JFX/ICDX
- **Crude Oil (WTI/Brent):** Rolling spot or futures via commodity brokers
- **Forex as Commodity Futures:** Treated as commodity derivative under BAPPEBTI rules

### Physical Gold Platforms
| Platform | Type | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| **Pluang (Gold)** | Digital Gold | Backed by Antam; micro-investing; buy/sell in IDR |
| **Tamasia / Tamasia Syariah** | Digital Gold | Sharia-compliant gold savings; backed by physical gold |
| **Tokopedia / Shopee (Emas)** | Marketplace Gold | Partner with gold providers; verify backing |
| **Pegadaian (Emas)** | State-Owned | Physical and digital gold; widely trusted |
| **Antam (Logam Mulia)** | Bullion Dealer | Physical gold bars; premium product |

### Index Futures / Single Stock Futures
- BEI offers **Index Futures (LQ45)** and some single-stock futures via its derivatives division, but retail participation is limited compared to regional markets.
- Most retail "index trading" in Indonesia is done via CFDs (offshore) or mutual fund/ETF investing.

## 10. Methodology & Data Quality Disclaimer

This document was compiled from publicly known platform lists, regulatory frameworks, and market structure knowledge. The following items should be independently verified by the user:

1. **Current BAPPEBTI license status** for any broker or exchange. Licenses can be revoked or suspended.
2. **Current fee schedules.** Zero-commission and low-fee offerings change frequently.
3. **Account opening acceptance.** International broker policies on Indonesian tax residents shift based on compliance and CRS/FATCA considerations.
4. **Crypto exchange operational status.** Some registered exchanges may have paused operations or merged.
5. **Minimum deposit requirements.** Fintech platforms often lower these over time.

**Recommendation:** Before committing funds, verify the platform's current regulatory standing directly with the regulator's online database (not just the platform's marketing claims).
